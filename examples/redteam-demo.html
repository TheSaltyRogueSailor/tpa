TPA: Triple Protection Authentication

Red-team demo: one legit AI-agent checkout, then five attack types. Generated 2026-10-24T17:38:56.309Z from a local, in-memory run of the real TPA service.
1. Signed mandate
The server signs the exact checkout: merchant, items, shipping, tax, total.
2. Passkey approval
The owner approves that exact mandate with a passkey.
3. Exact match, once
At payment time the checkout must match, and the approval works once.

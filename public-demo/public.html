What TPA is

TPA sits between an agent and the money. A payment has to pass three checks:

  • Person. The owner approves with a passkey.
  • Presence. The agent is a known signer right now.
  • Permission. The cart matches exactly what was approved.

Approvals are single use. If the merchant or a price changes after approval, the payment is blocked. TPA is built on Rootstock. This demo does not deploy a contract and does not move money.
